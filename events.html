What's Happening at Riverbend

Beyond our regular class schedule, we host a handful of special sessions and community gatherings each month. Everyone's welcome—no experience required.

Free

Monthly Complimentary Session

First Saturday of every month · 10:00 AM

A free, all-levels class open to anyone curious about trying yoga for the first time—no mat or experience needed, just show up.

New

Late Evening Sessions

Fridays · 8:30 PM

A slower-paced, dimly lit practice designed to help you unwind at the end of the week before the weekend begins.

Seasonal

Full Moon Restorative Circle

Monthly, date varies with the lunar calendar · 7:00 PM

A candlelit restorative practice paired with guided breathwork and reflection, held on the night of the full moon.
