Our Story & Origins

Riverbend Yoga Studio was founded with a singular vision: to create a sacred, grounding sanctuary where individuals can pause, realign their energies, and reconnect with their inner selves. Established along the winding banks of the local river, the studio takes its name from the natural flow of the water—teaching us how to gracefully navigate life's currents through mindful movement and breath.

Meet the Founder

The studio was created by Elena Vance, a dedicated holistic practitioner and longtime instructor who spent over a decade traveling and studying alignment-based practices. After experiencing how transformative intentional breathwork and restorative asanas could be for personal healing, Elena sought to build a welcoming community space where students of all levels could find peace, physical strength, and spiritual balance.

Our Philosophy

Here at Riverbend, we believe that yoga is not just about physical postures, but a continuous journey of self-discovery. Whether you are seeking the grounding energy of a Vinyasa flow or the deep release of a Restorative session, our studio is designed to support your unique path toward holistic wellness.

Instructor Bios

Elena Vance has been teaching mindful movement for over a decade. Her teaching style prioritizes safe alignment, compassionate self-inquiry, and deep community connection.
